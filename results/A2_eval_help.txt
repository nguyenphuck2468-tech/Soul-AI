usage: eval.py [-h] [--seeds SEEDS [SEEDS ...]] [--steps STEPS]
               [--episodes EPISODES] [--env-steps ENV_STEPS]
               [--warmup-episodes WARMUP_EPISODES]
               [--updates-per-collect UPDATES_PER_COLLECT]
               [--plan-samples PLAN_SAMPLES] [--out OUT]

Evaluate Soul against fixed Pendulum controllers.

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --seeds SEEDS [SEEDS ...]
  --steps STEPS         exact number of optimizer updates (default: 3000)
  --episodes EPISODES
  --env-steps ENV_STEPS
  --warmup-episodes WARMUP_EPISODES
  --updates-per-collect UPDATES_PER_COLLECT
  --plan-samples PLAN_SAMPLES
                        stochastic collection planner candidates; 0 uses the
                        actor directly
  --out OUT
